Make it polite

On the phone, start politely. Then the word order changes.
1
When does it open?  →  Could you tell me when it ?
2
Where is the office?  →  Could you tell me where the office ?
3
Do I need to book?  →  Do you know I need to book?
4
How much does it cost?  →  Could you tell me how much it ?
Polite voice: start a little high, then up and down at the end. Smile. People can hear a smile!
Term 4  ·  Explore community services
Pairs say the polite question first, then click the blank. Point out: no do, does or did after the question word, and Do you know IF for yes / no questions. Drill one or two with a polite voice and a flat voice, and ask the class which sounds better. This previews Stop 4 (Activities 12 to 14), so keep it short: recognition only.
